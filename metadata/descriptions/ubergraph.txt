Ubergraph (ubergraph) is an RDF triplestore that integrates a suite of OBO Foundry ontologies into one graph with precomputed inferred relationships, developed by the INCATools community. It loads 63 ontology files, among them Uberon anatomy, the Cell Ontology, the Gene Ontology, MONDO, the Human and Mammalian Phenotype Ontologies, NCBI Taxonomy, the NCI Thesaurus, the Protein Ontology, the Sequence Ontology, ENVO, FoodOn, PATO and the Relation Ontology, plus bridge files between species-specific anatomy ontologies; ChEBI terms are present too. A reasoner has already materialised the entailed rdfs:subClassOf and relation edges, so a single triple pattern returns a term's complete ancestors or descendants without a property path, giving logically complete answers for a subset of OWL. With about 600 million triples, it is the hierarchy the other graphs in the federation lean on: their ontology identifiers can be expanded into whole subtrees here, and this server's expand_ontology_term tool and 51 of its crosswalks use it. The Disease Ontology (DOID) is not included, so expand disease terms through MONDO.
