Wikidata (wikidata) is the free, collaboratively edited knowledge base run by the Wikimedia Foundation, readable and editable by both humans and machines. It describes items of every kind, from people, places and organisations to genes, proteins, chemicals, diseases, taxa and publications. Each item has a Q identifier and is described by statements that use properties with P identifiers, many with qualifiers and references. The copy in the OKN federation holds about 20.9 billion triples, some 2.3 billion distinct subjects and more than 61,000 properties, and the registry notes that it is slightly out of date relative to the standalone QLever Wikidata endpoint. Wikidata links many of its items to external identifiers such as NCBI Gene, Ensembl, HGNC, MeSH and UMLS, which makes it a bridge between graphs that key the same entities differently; four of this server's crosswalks use it to convert gene identifiers and to map MeSH to UMLS. It is too large for this server to enumerate a schema, so queries should start from known items or properties.
