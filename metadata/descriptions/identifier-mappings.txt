ID Mappings (identifier-mappings) is a subset of Wikidata that links Wikidata entities to the same entities in external identifier systems. It was produced by taking the external-identifier statements in the Wikidata RDF dump and converting Wikidata's own predicates to standard ones: nearly all of its roughly 26 million triples are skos:exactMatch, with a few thousand owl:equivalentClass, owl:equivalentProperty and skos:narrowMatch links. The subjects are about 14.8 million Wikidata items, plus some 55,000 lexemes and a few properties. The targets span many domains: proteins and taxa (UniProt, the largest target with about 2.8 million links, plus IPNI, iNaturalist and TDWG identifiers), researchers and publications (ORCID, DBLP, Open Library), chemicals and genes (PubChem, Ensembl), the NCI Thesaurus, library authorities (Library of Congress, BnF, WorldCat FAST, Getty vocabularies), places (GNIS, OpenStreetMap) and music (MusicBrainz). It lets a query move between a Wikidata entity and its external identifiers without touching the full Wikidata graph.
