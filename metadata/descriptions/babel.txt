Babel KG (babel) is an RDF rendering of Babel, the identifier-equivalence dataset of NCATS's Biomedical Data Translator. Babel groups identifiers from different vocabularies that name the same concept into cliques, following Biolink Model conventions, so that, for example, a MeSH identifier and a DrugBank identifier for water are recognised as the same entity. Each member points to its clique's preferred identifier with skos:exactMatch, and only the preferred identifier carries an rdfs:label and a biolink:category. Cliques cover genes (NCBI Gene, Ensembl, HGNC), proteins (UniProt, PR), diseases and phenotypes (MONDO, DOID, HP, OMIM, MeSH, UMLS, MedGen, SNOMED CT) and chemicals (ChEBI, DrugBank, ChEMBL, PubChem, CAS, InChIKey, UNII and others); gene and protein cliques are kept separate, so babel does not map a gene to its protein. With about 1.5 billion triples and no domain data of its own, babel serves as the middle hop of a join between two graphs that key the same concept under different identifier schemes; 29 of this server's crosswalks run through it.
